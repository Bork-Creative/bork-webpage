3. Information We Collect

We and our service providers may collect the following categories of information, depending on how you use the Game, your device settings, your consent choices, and the SDKs enabled for your region and platform.

a. Account and Gameplay Data

  • Player progress and game data: In-game progress, achievements, settings, inventory, scores, and related gameplay state may be stored locally and/or through Unity Gaming Services Cloud or other backend services.
  • Account information: If you use player authentication or link the Game to Apple Game Center, Google Play Games, or another supported account service, we may receive identifiers and basic profile information, such as a player ID, display name, or authentication token needed to sync progress and provide account features.
  • Support communications: If you contact us, we may process your email address, message contents, device details you provide, and other information needed to respond.

b. Device, Technical, Analytics, and Performance Data

  • Device and app data: Device model, operating system, app version, language, country or region inferred from device settings or IP address, network type, and similar technical information.
  • Identifiers: App instance IDs, device identifiers, advertising identifiers where permitted, vendor identifiers, IP address, and identifiers generated by SDK providers.
  • Usage and events: Gameplay events, session length, screens or features used, progression events, level attempts, rewards, purchases, ad interactions, and other in-game activity.
  • Diagnostics: Crash logs, error reports, performance data, and other diagnostics used to maintain and improve stability.

c. Unity Gaming Services Data

We use Unity Gaming Services to operate game features including player authentication, cloud saves, analytics, diagnostics, remote configuration, cloud code, push notifications, cloud content delivery, and purchase-related backend functionality. Unity Gaming Services may process player identifiers, authentication data, gameplay progress, device and app information, analytics events, diagnostics and crash data, purchase-related events, device tokens needed to send push notifications, remote configuration data, and content delivery request metadata.

d. Advertising, Attribution, and Measurement Data

If ads, ad mediation, attribution, or measurement are enabled, we and our partners may collect and process:

  • Ad requests, impressions, clicks, completed views, rewarded ad events, revenue estimates, impression-level revenue data, and other ad delivery events.
  • Advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, approximate location, app usage events, and consent or privacy flags.
  • Purchase-related and attribution events used to understand ad campaign performance, in-app purchase performance, ad revenue, and fraud prevention.
  • Open Measurement and third-party measurement signals used to verify ad viewability, ad delivery, and ad performance.

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • To operate the Game and provide gameplay, progress saving, account linking, rewards, purchases, and support.
  • To store, sync, and restore player progress and settings.
  • To provide player authentication, cloud saves, remote configuration, cloud code, push notifications, cloud content delivery, and purchase-related backend functionality through Unity Gaming Services.

  • To process consent, opt-out, age, privacy, and platform signals and pass those signals to partners where required.
  • To enforce our terms, protect users and services, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

For users in the European Union, United Kingdom, EFTA/EEA, and similar jurisdictions, our legal bases for processing may include:

  • Contractual necessity: To provide the Game, store progress, support account linking, deliver purchases, and operate requested features.
  • Consent: For ad personalization, certain analytics, attribution, measurement, and partner processing where consent is legally required.
  • Legitimate interests: To maintain security, prevent fraud, diagnose errors, improve the Game, provide contextual ads, and understand aggregate usage, where these interests are not overridden by your rights.

7. Consent, Opt-Out, and Privacy Choices

Where legally required, including in the European Union, United Kingdom, EFTA/EEA, California, and other applicable regions, we request consent before enabling personalized advertising or other partner processing that requires consent. We also pass consent, age, "Do Not Sell or Share", limit ad tracking, restricted processing, or similar privacy signals to advertising, mediation, analytics, attribution, and measurement partners as required by law, platform policy, and partner policy.

Your choices may include:

  • Managing consent in the Game's privacy or consent settings, where available.
  • Resetting or limiting your mobile advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Using Apple App Tracking Transparency controls on iOS where applicable.
  • Using Google advertising privacy controls on Android where applicable.
  • Opting out of sale, sharing, cross-context behavioral advertising, targeted advertising, or personalized advertising where required by California or other U.S. state privacy laws.
  • Contacting us to exercise privacy rights listed in this Privacy Policy.

Opting out of personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all ads. You may still receive contextual ads, frequency-capped ads, or ads needed to support rewarded ad features.
